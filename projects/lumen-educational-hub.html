Overview

LUMEN Educational Hub is the online learning platform for Lumen International Academy. It gives learners a single place to discover courses, study at their own pace, join live classes and track their progress towards a verifiable certificate.

The platform is built around a concept-first teaching model: pre-recorded lessons, live problem clinics with instructors, continuous quizzes and tutor-marked assignments, and protected in-app learning resources.

The problem

The academy needed to reach learners far beyond a physical classroom without losing the structure, feedback and academic support that make teaching effective. Off-the-shelf course marketplaces could not host live classes, continuous assessment and protected resources in one coherent experience.

The solution

A complete learning platform with a public marketing site, a searchable and filterable course catalogue, enrolment and payment flows, a lesson interface for structured study, a student dashboard for progress, instructor tools for publishing and marking, and administrative control over courses, pricing and learners.

Key features

  • Public site with course, instructor and pricing information
  • Course catalogue with search, category, level, price and sort filters
  • Structured lessons with pre-recorded video and protected in-app resources
  • Live online classes and problem clinics alongside self-paced study
  • Continuous quizzes and tutor-marked assignments with real feedback
  • Student dashboard covering enrolments, progress and certificates
  • Instructor publishing and marking workflows
  • Admin control over courses, publishing state, pricing and learners
  • Account creation, sign-in and enrolment / payment flow
  • Verifiable certificates on successful completion
